# Soundtrack 02 — Shadowline

## Purpose

Shadowline is a new instrumental early-1980s synth-pop arrangement of Cole Porter’s **“Love for Sale”** using the underlying 1930 composition, which entered the U.S. public domain on January 1, 2026. Night Signal remains unchanged.

The linked recordings are production-feeling references only. No recording audio, performance, later reharmonization, modern arrangement, or production-specific part is sampled or transcribed into the game.

## Source and rights boundary

The arrangement is derived from the original 1930 T. B. Harms voice-and-piano score, plate 8591:

- [IMSLP catalog](https://imslp.org/wiki/Love_for_Sale_%28Porter%2C_Cole%29)
- [1930 score scan](https://ks15.imslp.org/files/imglnks/usimg/a/a3/IMSLP1009919-PMLP1570857-Porter,_New_Yorkers,_Love_For_Sale.pdf)
- [U.S. Copyright Office](https://copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/)

The composition’s U.S. public-domain status does not imply that particular recordings or modern arrangements are public domain. International protection may differ in life-plus-70 territories.

## Arrangement language

- 112 bars at 114 BPM, straight 4/4 rather than the score’s cut-time theatrical feel.
- Instrumental only; no lyrics or pseudo-vocal samples.
- Preserve the score’s defining B-flat major/minor ambiguity and chromatic inner motion.
- Render the score-derived melodic line as a narrow Prophet-style pulse lead.
- Build the Prophet-family timbre from two thin, differently proportioned pulse waves plus a restrained saw component; use slight oscillator detune and approximately 0.31 Hz pitch modulation.
- Shape the patch with a dark resonant low-pass, a pronounced filter-envelope decay, low amp sustain, short release, stereo chorus, and restrained cross-fed plate-like ambience.
- Bright, close, humanized drum one-shots with a separately articulated Synare-like accent.
- Bass follows the composition’s long tonic/IV fields while adding original octave and turnaround articulation.
- Restrained guitar answers and original chord-stab countermelodies provide the new arrangement layer.
- No material is taken from the supplied YouTube recording’s master or performance.

## Procedural score map

The compact 112-bar MIDI/chord map in `audio/music-track-02-score-data.ts` contains the lead contour and restrained major, minor, seventh, sixth, and diminished Prophet voicings. Playback remains entirely procedural Web Audio at 114 BPM.

## Form

- Bars 1–8: new filtered introduction built from the refrain’s repeated-note cell.
- Bars 9–24: first A statement; melody appears over the E-flat/B-flat-minor color shift.
- Bars 25–40: second A statement with bass and guitar development.
- Bars 41–56: bridge harmony; lead thins out while synth voicing and harmonic rhythm broaden.
- Bars 57–72: third A statement with stronger drums.
- Bars 73–88: new instrumental development of public-domain melodic fragments.
- Bars 89–104: final A return with the fullest lead and guitar support.
- Bars 105–112: new loop-out derived from the composition’s falling cadence.
