# Soundtrack 04 — Midnight Vector

## Identity

Midnight Vector is a procedural Web Audio composition shaped by a supplied musical profile. It adopts broad traits—100 BPM, D-minor gravity, syncopated sixteenths, sliding sub bass, and nocturnal space—without reproducing a source melody or recording.

## Composition

- Tempo: 100 BPM, 4/4.
- Form: 32 bars / 76.8 seconds.
- Tonal center: D minor, with open G and C colors.
- Low contour: F–D–C–D / F–D–G–G, varied by phrase and reinforced by selected octaves at the peak.
- Upper ostinato: a narrow D-minor figure introduced only in the second half.

## Procedural orchestration

- Saturated mono sine/triangle bass with asymmetric portamento.
- Synthesized kick, shell/body drum, and velocity-shaped hats on a syncopated sixteenth grid.
- Band-limited pulse ostinato whose filter follows vehicle speed.
- Four drifting sine voices for a wide dark pad.
- Short cross-channel delay and filtered procedural air; no PCM or prerecorded media.

## Form

- Bars 1–8: filtered bass pulse and sparse atmosphere.
- Bars 9–16: syncopated drums enter.
- Bars 17–24: wide pad and upper ostinato develop.
- Bars 25–30: octave bass reinforcement, denser hats, and full peak.
- Bars 31–32: percussion and ostinato strip away into the loop.

## Adaptive behavior

Speed opens the ostinato and pad. Drift reduces upper-synth presence for tire readability. Higher Chase tiers increase hat subdivisions. Collision, capture, and reset messages use the standard soundtrack duck and synthesized body-drum punctuation. Existing drums, bass, synth, atmosphere, and guitar stem controls remain compatible; this cue intentionally leaves the guitar stem empty.
